My Dream Destination

Shillong (Meghalaya)

Although I have been to Meghalaya twice. Once recently and before that I lived there for a year. But that place always amazes me. Whether it is the urban Shillong City, Police Bazar area or rural areas where internet is not available. Many issues like navigation and other things then there is the weather (foggy, rainy, windy, misty) and that is the beauty of Meghalaya. The Scotland of the East. I have been to many places in Meghalaya but there are still many places which I want to visit. Some of them are Mawlynnong Village, Mawsynram, Dawki, Nongriat Village, etc. I have seen one season but the people khasi tribe locals told me. Meghalaya have 3 seasons of beauty. It is like a person whom you saw in 3 different moods, clothes or say looks. I have seen the beauty of Meghalaya in rainy season and winter season. But I want to see it in summer season as well. The beauty of Meghalaya is not only in its natural beauty but also in its people, culture, food, and lifestyle. I want to explore more of this beautiful state and learn more about its rich heritage and traditions.
